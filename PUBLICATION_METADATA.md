## Short description

A frozen conditional two-phase holographic cosmology in which a published gravitational O(4) bounce continues to an early dS3 wall phase, a phenomenological handoff history fixes a global screen ruler, and a maximal-depth reconstruction rule maps that ruler to a late global/extrinsic dS4 geometry with exact w=-1.
