# Future derivation roadmap from the frozen two-phase theory

This roadmap is **not required for v1.0.0 internal consistency**. Its purpose is to progressively replace conditional/phenomenological elements with derivations while leaving the frozen architecture unchanged unless a falsification forces reopening.

## Priority A — derive the handoff / history datum N_*

### Goal
Replace the sole continuous background history datum `N_*` with an action- or geometry-derived transition criterion.

### Highest-value route: material-flow completion
Investigate whether the O(4)/4D superfluid substrate carries a nontrivial stationary flow field around/through the bubble interface even though the wall profile itself is static.

Required tests:

1. derive the conserved current/stress tensor from the existing material/superfluid action;
2. impose O(4) Euclidean and O(3)/dS3 Lorentzian symmetry carefully;
3. distinguish symmetry-compatible normal/radial or higher-dimensional flow from literal wall rotation;
4. determine whether the flow develops a global invariant, flux threshold, critical Mach/sound condition, or topology change capable of selecting the handoff;
5. test whether the same flow appears in the previously considered 5D-black-hole / 4D-bulk phase-transition picture;
6. check whether a derived flow threshold lands near the frozen `N_*` without using late-DE data.

A successful result upgrades the largest remaining background phenomenological datum toward FP.

## Priority B — derive the screen-scale -> IR-depth theorem

### Goal
Derive the currently discrete maximal-depth reconstruction law and its order-one normalization.

Tasks:

1. derive the Wilsonian/DtN split of local microscopic response versus global screen response in the complete gravity+wall system;
2. identify the exact invariant global screen ruler entering the reconstruction map;
3. derive whether maximal-depth saturation follows from entropy, code-subspace capacity, Brown–York positivity, causal wedge completion, or another gravitational theorem;
4. fix the order-one coefficient without late-DE input;
5. test whether the resulting `L_IR` is stable under thick-wall corrections and allowed renormalization choices.

Success would turn the late absolute curvature scale from conditional into a prediction once `N_*` is also derived.

## Priority C — derive primordial A_s and n_s

### Goal
Replace the prepared Gaussian state `{A_s,n_s}` with a microscopic/holographic derivation.

Retained clues:

- exact Goldstone loop gives the correct nonlocal `|q|^3` scalar shape;
- a global relational clock exists;
- 5D parent gravity has a nonzero scalar projector;
- actual O(4) matter flow exists but is too weak/narrow in the present reduction;
- a yet-unmodeled physical material/reconstruction flow may combine the correct scalar ownership with gravitational normalization.

Preferred program:

1. derive the full constrained wavefunctional of the flowing 4D/5D substrate around the static O(4) wall;
2. compute its Brown–York trace form factor `B(q)` without CMB input;
3. determine whether flow/reconstruction converts the Goldstone or another existing mode into an adiabatic classical curvature perturbation;
4. derive amplitude, tilt, running and non-Gaussianity;
5. only after the scalar sector is fixed, derive the tensor kernel and `r`.

Do not add a new inflaton or large-N sector unless the frozen architecture is independently falsified.

## Priority D — exact handoff energy partition and reheating

### Goal
Turn the controlled stress-weight reheating family into a quantitative prediction.

Tasks:

1. derive the boundary-time profile of the dimensional quench;
2. compute channel-resolved Bogoliubov coefficients into visible, flavor, Phi/Goldstone, gravitational and DM sectors;
3. derive the visible reheating temperature and dark-radiation contribution;
4. confirm that the universal geometric handoff preserves the Phase-B2 flavor invariants beyond the leading factorization theorem;
5. verify baryon/DM yield bookkeeping through the same transition without double counting boundary/bulk energy.

## Priority E — DM/memory independent completion

Keep `H8` and `Lambda_mem` strictly in the DM/memory branch. Revisit their microscopic origin only after the frozen two-phase cosmology is kept intact. Determine whether the surviving transient pair-instability theorem can be useful for DM state preparation even though it failed as a DE mechanism.

## Priority F — full material-flow / black-hole analogy audit

The user's physical intuition that a material 4D/5D substrate should flow around a static bubble interface is retained as a dedicated future program.

The analogy to matter near a black hole is heuristic only. A valid derivation must answer:

- what quantity is flowing;
- along which dimension(s);
- whether the flow is normal, radial, tangential, vortical, or topological;
- how O(4)/O(3) symmetry is preserved;
- whether angular momentum/vorticity is actually zero, as presently expected;
- whether the flow stress modifies Israel/CDL junction conditions;
- whether the flow supplies the handoff trigger, reconstruction beta function, primordial scalar response, or none of these.

The frozen model assumes **no rotating wall** and no observable global vorticity.

## Priority G — eventual empirical predictions

After A-C are improved, derive or sharpen:

- `N_*` and reheating temperature;
- scalar running and non-Gaussianity;
- tensor amplitude/spectrum;
- residual curvature/topology signatures;
- dark-radiation prediction;
- any late holographic deviations from exact LambdaCDM beyond `w=-1`;
- consistency relations linking early wall parameters to late `L_IR`.

## Reopening rule

A future derivation may replace a conditional assumption with a theorem. It should not alter the two-phase architecture unless:

1. the derivation produces a mathematical inconsistency with the frozen structure; or
2. a robust observation decisively falsifies a frozen prediction/condition.
